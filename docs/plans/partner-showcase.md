# Partner / sponsor showcase — plan (save for later)

> Status: outline approved (2026-06-25). **Sponsor tiers deferred** at Johnson's request. Not built.

## Purpose
The site is a credibility / sales asset for (1) potential **hosts/guests** and (2) **sponsors**.
Productize the existing `/brands/[id]` pages as the demonstrable benefit — the website demos itself.

Two audiences, one shared asset:
- **Hosts:** a permanent, beautiful, SEO brand page + embedded episode = earned-media asset they're proud to share.
- **Sponsors:** endorsement + placement, in tiers. *(Tiers deferred.)*

## A. `/partner` (or `/for-brands`) page
1. **Hook** — one line each: be a guest / become a sponsor.
2. **What your brand gets** — permanent page, map pin, embedded episode, SEO backlink, shareable Visit route; each item links to a **real live example** as proof.
3. **Tiers comparison** — ⏸️ DEFERRED (later).
4. **Social proof** — featured-brand logos.
5. **CTA** — into the apply/sponsor form. *(Depends on the inbound-forms fix — this page is the top of a funnel that currently dead-ends; see `inbound-forms.md`.)*

## B. Personalized live preview (the "wow" play)
Because content is just sheet rows: add a prospect as a **draft row → build → send a private link to their finished page** ("here's your brand on Storys"). A live personalized mockup beats a slide; the architecture makes it nearly free.

## C. "As heard on Storys" badge
Embeddable badge/snippet partners put on their own site, linking back to their Storys page. Backlink both ways = SEO for both + free distribution.

## Sequencing
- This page is the **deliberate exception** to the Phase-1 visual gate — sponsor-facing polish converts directly to revenue, so a Tier-1 "bolder" slice is justified here first.
- **v0:** plain partner page (framing + 2–3 example links + working CTA).
- **v1:** "As heard on" badge + (tiers, when un-deferred).
- **v2:** personalized live preview.

## Depends on
Inbound forms (the CTA target). Otherwise independent of Phase 1.
